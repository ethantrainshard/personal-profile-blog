CloudForge is a multi-cloud infrastructure provisioning platform that enables teams to deploy and manage resources across AWS, Azure, and GCP from a single interface. It enforces policy-as-code to ensure all deployments comply with organizational standards and security requirements.

The tool addresses the complexity of multi-cloud environments where each provider has its own API, terminology, and best practices. CloudForge abstracts these differences into a unified configuration language while maintaining provider-specific optimizations.
